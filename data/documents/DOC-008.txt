1. SERVICE TIER DEFINITIONS AND REPLICATION TOPOLOGY
StratosVault Tier-0 Sovereign Object Storage ("Tier-0") provides geo-redundant immutable storage for regulated financial and defense workloads within the Alpine Sovereign Zone. Every object written to a Tier-0 bucket is synchronously erasure-coded across three underground data centers located in Lucerne (Site Alpha), Bern (Site Beta), and Lugano (Site Gamma) before an HTTP 201 Write Acknowledgement is returned to the client.

By contrast, StratosVault Tier-1 Standard Storage replicates data asynchronously across only two sites (Lucerne and Bern) with a replication lag window of up to 90 seconds, and StratosVault Cold-Archive Deep Vault stores magnetic tape shards in a single facility in Andermatt.

2. CRYPTOGRAPHIC ENCRYPTION & KEY ROTATION MANDATES
All Tier-0 objects undergo envelope encryption prior to disk commit:
- Data-at-rest payload blocks are encrypted using AES-256-GCM combined with a post-quantum ML-KEM-1024 key encapsulation wrapper.
- Customer Master Keys (CMKs) reside inside FIPS 140-3 Level 4 Hardware Security Modules (HSMs) manufactured by SecuroSys.
- Root Key-Encryption Keys (KEKs) are rotated automatically every 90 calendar days, whereas per-bucket Data-Encryption Keys (DEKs) are rotated every 24 hours or after encrypting 500 GB of data, whichever occurs first.

If a tenant initiates an emergency cryptographic shred ("Crypto-Erase"), all HSM-held key shares across Sites Alpha, Beta, and Gamma are zeroized within 18 seconds, rendering stored ciphertext permanently unrecoverable.

3. AVAILABILITY GUARANTEE, RTO/RPO, AND SERVICE CREDIT SCHEDULE
StratosVault guarantees a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.995% for Tier-0 read and write operations (corresponding to no more than 2.16 minutes of unplanned downtime per 30-day billing month). For Tier-0 workloads, the Recovery Point Objective (RPO) is 0 seconds (zero data loss) and the Recovery Time Objective (RTO) for automatic site failover is 15 seconds.

If Monthly Uptime Percentage for a Tier-0 bucket falls below 99.995% in any billing month, the customer is eligible for Service Credits applied against the next monthly invoice:
- Uptime between 99.95% and 99.994%: 15% Service Credit
- Uptime between 99.50% and 99.949%: 35% Service Credit
- Uptime below 99.50%: 100% Service Credit

To receive a Service Credit, the tenant must file a support claim in the StratosVault Governance Portal within 14 calendar days after the end of the billing month in which the outage occurred.

4. RETRIEVAL LATENCY AND RETENTION LOCKS
First-byte read latency (TTFB) for Tier-0 objects up to 64 MB is guaranteed at or below 12 ms at the 99th percentile (P99). By comparison, Cold-Archive Deep Vault retrieval requires a 3.5-hour robotic tape mount and staging window. Objects placed under Compliance WORM (Write-Once-Read-Many) Retention Lock have a minimum lock duration of 30 days and a maximum lock duration of 35 years; locked objects cannot be deleted even by the tenant's Root Administrator prior to lock expiration.
