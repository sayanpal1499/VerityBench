DOCUMENT ID: DOC-002
TITLE: Aethelgard Orbital Systems — Global Travel, Remote Work & Expense Policy (Policy Pol-HR-409)
EFFECTIVE DATE: March 1, 2026
APPROVED BY: Chief People Officer Henrik Vestergaard

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy governs work-location eligibility, domestic and international business travel, meal reimbursement, and home-office stipends for all full-time salaried employees of Aethelgard Orbital Systems. External contractors, summer interns, and part-time advisors are excluded from these allowances and must follow Schedule B of their vendor agreements.

2. HYBRID AND REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY
Full-time employees in Software Engineering, Mission Analytics, and Legal Compliance may work remotely for up to 3 days per week, provided they attend in-person core collaboration days at their assigned hub every Tuesday and Thursday. By contrast, Propulsion Test Engineers and Cleanroom Technicians are classified as Mission-Critical On-Site Staff and are not eligible for regular remote work.

Employees in Hybrid-Eligible roles may request a temporary "Work From Anywhere" (WFA) allocation of up to 14 consecutive calendar days per fiscal year. Every WFA request must be submitted through the Portal-X HR system at least 21 calendar days prior to departure and requires written sign-off from both the employee's direct Director and the Export Control Office.

3. BUSINESS TRAVEL & FLIGHT BOOKING RULES
All commercial air travel must be booked through the corporate Navan-Aethelgard portal at least 10 days before departure. Economy Class is mandatory for all single-leg flights with a scheduled gate-to-gate duration under 7 hours. Employees traveling on single-leg international flights of 7 hours or longer are authorized to book Premium Economy. Business Class airfare is restricted strictly to Vice Presidents and above, or to employees holding a documented medical accommodation approved by Occupational Health.

4. LODGING AND DAILY MEAL PER DIEM CAPS
Aethelgard reimburses actual reasonable hotel nightly rates (excluding room taxes) up to the following regional caps:
- Tier-1 High-Cost Cities (Zurich, Tokyo, New York, London, Singapore): $310 USD per night
- Tier-2 Standard Metro Areas (all other national capitals and regional hubs): $215 USD per night
- Tier-3 Field Test Sites (Kiruna Launch Range and Mojave Annex): $145 USD per night

Daily meal and incidental expenses are reimbursed up to a flat cap of $85 USD per day in Tier-1 cities and $60 USD per day in Tier-2 and Tier-3 locations. Alcohol, mini-bar purchases, in-room movies, and airline lounge day passes are strictly non-reimbursable under all tiers.

5. HOME-OFFICE EQUIPMENT STIPEND & EXPENSE SUBMISSION DEADLINES
Newly hired Hybrid-Eligible full-time employees receive a one-time home-office ergonomic stipend of $650 USD upon completing their 90-day probationary period. All itemized receipts over $25 USD must be uploaded to the Concur-Aethelgard ledger within 30 calendar days of the transaction date. Claims submitted between 31 and 60 days after the transaction incur a mandatory 15% administrative late deduction, and any expense claim submitted more than 60 calendar days after the transaction date is automatically forfeited without exception.
